Organizers

Odej Kao

Technische Universität Berlin

Odej is a full professor of distributed and operating systems at the Technische Universität Berlin. His research expertise includes LLMOps/AIOps, fault tolerance and reliability for large infrastructures, workflows / streaming analytics, and cloud computing. Odej is involved as a PI in BIFOLD (National Institute on Foundations of Learning and Data), Collaborative Research Centre FONDA (Foundations of Workflows for Large-Scale Scientific Data Analysis), and BBDC (National Big Data Centre). Prof. Kao is a member of numerous program and review committees and has published over 500 papers in peer-reviewed proceedings and journals. He organized more than 10 workshops and co-organized the 19th edition of the International Conference on Service Oriented Computing (ICSOC 2021) and the 37th International Symposium on Computer Architecture and High-Performance Computing (SBAC-PAD 2025). He was also a guest editor for several special issues and a member of the ParCo editorial board.

Li Shang

Fudan University

Li is a tenured professor and Ph.D. advisor at the College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Fudan University. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Engineering from Princeton University and has long conducted research at the intersection of computer systems and artificial intelligence. His primary research interests include machine learning systems, embodied intelligence, very-large-scale integration (VLSI) design, and electronic design automation (EDA). He has published more than 200 research papers in these areas, which have received multiple Best Paper Awards and nominations and more than 10,000 citations. He is a recipient of the U.S. NSF CAREER Award. He has served on the Technical Program Committees (TPCs) of more than 10 leading conferences in computer systems and machine learning.

Google Scholar

Philipp Petersen

University of Vienna

Philipp is an Associate Professor of Mathematics of Machine Learning at the Faculty of Mathematics, University of Vienna. His research focuses on the foundations of deep learning, with expertise in approximation theory, statistical learning theory, optimization of deep neural networks, and numerical analysis. He has organized several international scientific events, including the 3rd Conference on Compressed Sensing and its Applications and workshop on Wavelet and Tensor Methods for Partial Differential Equations. He further organized symposia such on theoretical foundations of deep learning, uncertainty quantification, and sampling and learning of Deep Neural Networks. He is Associate Editor for Mathematical Foundations of Computing and guest editor for the special issue “Sampling Theory, Signal Processing, and Data Analysis.” He regularly serves as reviewer for leading journals and conferences in machine learning and applied mathematics.
